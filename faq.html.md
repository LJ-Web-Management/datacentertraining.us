# Common Questions

FAQ

## Programs & Curriculum

**What's the difference between Comprehensive Programs and Modular Specializations?**

Comprehensive Programs are multi-module courses (8 to 16 hours) covering a full discipline in depth, like data center design or operations management. Modular Specializations are focused 1-8 hour courses on a single skill, like UPS load testing or fiber termination. Many teams combine one comprehensive program with several modular courses for their role, which is exactly how our bundles are put together.

**How long does each course take?**

Comprehensive Programs run 8 to 16 hours; Modular Specializations run 1-8 hours. All courses are self-paced, so learners can start and stop and pick up where they left off. A certificate is issued immediately on completion.

**What topics does the course catalog cover?**

The catalog spans six tracks: Power & Electrical, Cooling & Facilities Efficiency, Operations & Reliability, Security & Compliance, Design, Planning & Commissioning, and Monitoring & Smart Facility. Together they cover 43 courses and 192 hours of training.

**Do I need a background in data center design or operations to take these courses?**

No prior certification is required. Comprehensive Programs assume basic familiarity with facility systems, which suits engineers, technicians, and managers already working in or around a data center. Modular Specializations are built for hands-on technicians and typically require no prerequisite beyond the relevant equipment exposure.

**Are course materials updated when codes or standards change?**

Yes. Course content is reviewed against the current versions of the standards and frameworks it references, such as ANSI/TIA-942, NFPA 70E, and the Uptime Institute Tier framework, so the training stays aligned with current practice.

**Can I see a syllabus or module list before enrolling?**

Yes. Every course page lists its full module breakdown, learning outcomes, and audience under "What You'll Learn" and "Course Modules," so you know exactly what's covered before you enroll.

## Certificates & Credentials

**Are these courses accepted as certification or a professional credential?**

No. These are knowledge and best-practices courses, not certification exams or licensing programs. Learners receive a certificate of completion documenting the training they finished. Our parent company, HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC, is an IACET Accredited Provider, but that accreditation is held by the company. These data center courses do not carry IACET CEUs and are not a professional certification.

**What standards and frameworks do the courses reference?**

Course content references the industry standards relevant to each topic: ANSI/TIA-942 and ISO 14644 for design, the Uptime Institute TIER framework for infrastructure benchmarking, NFPA 70E for electrical safety, and SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and PCI-DSS for security and compliance, among others. Each course lists the frameworks it covers directly on its course page.

**Do data center training certificates expire or need to be renewed?**

Certificates of completion don't have a fixed expiration date, but many employers require periodic refreshers as part of internal training programs, especially for safety-adjacent topics like electrical safety and access control. Check your employer's refresher policy; courses are short enough to retake as often as your program requires.

**How do I verify an employee's completed certificate?**

Each certificate of completion carries a unique certificate number and issue date. Enter them on HAZWOPER OSHA Training's [certificate verification page](https://hazwoper-osha.com/certificate-verification). A successful lookup confirms that the learner named on the certificate enrolled in, completed, and passed the course listed on it. It does not confirm that the person is qualified for specific work, licensed, or holds CEU credit. Those decisions stay with the employer.

**Do you offer CEUs or PDHs (continuing education units)?**

No. HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC, our parent company, is an IACET Accredited Provider, but these data center courses do not carry IACET CEUs. They are also not approved for PDHs or any other continuing education credit. Learners receive a certificate of completion only. Check with your licensing board or professional body if you specifically need CEU- or PDH-eligible coursework.

**Does a safety course qualify someone to do electrical or other hazardous work?**

No. An online course alone does not make anyone a qualified person for energized electrical work, confined space entry, refrigerant handling, or other hazardous tasks. Under OSHA rules and standards such as NFPA 70E, the employer decides who is qualified, based on training plus demonstrated skills on the specific equipment and tasks at that site. Our safety courses provide the knowledge portion of that training. Each safety course page lists the standards it covers and what it does and does not prepare a learner to do.

**How is the certificate delivered, and can I print it?**

Certificates of completion are issued digitally as soon as you finish a course and can be downloaded and printed for your records or an employer's training file.
