# Data Center Design Fundamentals

Comprehensive Program

Design data center facilities that scale — from site selection to redundancy strategy.

8 hrs · self-paced online Design & Planning ANSI/TIA-942

Every high-availability data center starts as a set of design decisions — and the wrong ones are expensive to unwind after concrete is poured. This course walks through the full design lifecycle used by enterprise facility teams: international standards (ANSI/TIA-942, ISO 14644), site selection criteria, thermal design principles, power architecture, and the redundancy trade-offs (N, N+1, N+2, 2N) that determine both uptime and capital cost.

You'll work through real design case studies, thermal modeling concepts, and ROI analysis frameworks used to justify facility investment to executive stakeholders. Rather than teaching a single vendor's playbook, the course focuses on the trade-off reasoning behind design choices, so you can evaluate proposals, vet vendor recommendations, and defend your own design decisions with data.

By the end, you'll be able to read a facility design proposal critically, model redundancy options against budget constraints, and build the financial case for an expansion or new build.

## Why This Matters in a Data Center

Most of a data center's lifetime cost and reliability is decided before construction starts. Site selection sets the risk from floods, power availability and fiber access. The power and cooling architecture sets how much redundancy the facility has and how easily it can grow. Changing any of these after the building is finished is slow and expensive.

Design teams work from shared references. ANSI/TIA-942 covers data center infrastructure including spaces, cabling and rated reliability levels, and ISO 14644 is often used to define air cleanliness targets. Redundancy models such as N+1 and 2N each trade cost against resilience. This program walks through the full design lifecycle: standards, site selection and planning, thermal design, power architecture, redundancy trade-offs and the financial modeling that supports expansion decisions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is ANSI/TIA-942?

ANSI/TIA-942 is the Telecommunications Industry Association standard for data center infrastructure. It covers the layout of spaces, cabling, and electrical, mechanical and other infrastructure, and defines rated reliability levels. Many owners and designers use it as the baseline for new facilities.

### What is the difference between N+1 and 2N redundancy?

N is the capacity needed to carry the load. N+1 adds one extra component, such as one spare UPS module or chiller. 2N provides two complete, independent systems, each able to carry the full load. 2N costs more but can tolerate the loss of an entire path.
