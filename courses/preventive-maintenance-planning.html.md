## Why This Matters in a Data Center

Without preventive maintenance, a data center runs until something fails, and in a critical facility that can be very costly. With too much maintenance, or poorly planned maintenance, the work itself becomes a risk, because many incidents happen while equipment is in maintenance mode or during the return to service.

A sustainable program balances scheduled preventive tasks with predictive techniques such as infrared thermography, vibration analysis and oil analysis, which show when equipment actually needs attention. It relies on good records in a CMMS. NFPA 70B, which became a mandatory standard in its 2023 edition, now requires an electrical maintenance program for covered equipment. This course covers equipment lifecycle, maintenance scheduling, predictive maintenance basics and documentation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between preventive and predictive maintenance?

Preventive maintenance is done on a fixed schedule, such as quarterly inspections. Predictive maintenance uses condition data, such as thermal imaging or vibration readings, to decide when maintenance is needed. Most strong programs combine both.

### What is NFPA 70B?

NFPA 70B covers maintenance of electrical equipment. In its 2023 edition it changed from a recommended practice to a standard, requiring an electrical maintenance program and setting maintenance intervals based on equipment condition.

### Why does maintenance documentation matter?

Records show what was done, what was found and when equipment is due next. They support trend analysis, warranty claims, audits and budgeting. The Documentation & Tracking module covers how to build this into a system your team will keep using.

### What is an equipment maintenance program?

It is the written system an organization uses to keep assets reliable: an equipment inventory, the tasks and intervals for each asset, who performs them, how work orders are scheduled and closed, and how results are recorded and reviewed. In a data center it also includes methods of procedure (MOPs) for maintaining live systems without causing an outage.
