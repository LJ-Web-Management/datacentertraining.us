## Why This Matters in a Data Center

Servers, storage arrays and switches are heavy, awkward and often installed high in a rack or low near the floor, in narrow aisles. Some modern GPU servers weigh well over 100 pounds. Lifting injuries, strains from reaching into racks and pinched fingers are some of the most common injuries in data center work.

The NIOSH Revised Lifting Equation is a widely used method for judging whether a lift is safe, and it starts from a recommended maximum of 51 pounds under ideal conditions. Real data center lifts are rarely ideal, so team lifts, server lifts and good planning matter. Workstation setup in operations centers matters too. This course covers safe lifting, ergonomic workstation setup, manual handling in tight spaces and injury prevention.

## Safety Scope & Limits

### Applicable standards and regulations

- No single OSHA standard covers ergonomics. OSHA addresses lifting and musculoskeletal hazards under the General Duty Clause, Section 5(a)(1) of the OSH Act.

### What this course does

- Covers safe lifting, team lifts, and ergonomic practices for racking equipment and other data hall work.

### What it does not do

- Does not replace your employer's material handling procedures, lift-equipment training, or medical guidance.

An online course alone does not make anyone qualified for hazardous work. Under OSHA rules, the employer decides who is qualified, based on training plus demonstrated skill on the specific equipment and tasks at your site. Referencing a standard does not mean the organization that publishes it has reviewed or endorsed this course.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How heavy is too heavy to lift alone?

The NIOSH Revised Lifting Equation starts from 51 pounds under ideal conditions and reduces that limit for lifts that are far from the body, high, low, twisting or frequent. Many rack-mount lifts fall well below that adjusted limit, so team lifts or mechanical lifts are often needed.

### When should a server lift be used?

For heavy equipment, installations high or low in the rack, and any lift where the worker cannot keep the load close to the body. Many facilities set a weight threshold above which a lift or second person is required.

### Who should take this course?

IT technicians, installation crews, remote hands staff and anyone who moves, racks or de-racks equipment in server rooms and data halls.
