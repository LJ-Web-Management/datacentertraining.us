## Why This Matters in a Data Center

Many data centers still use raised floors for airflow and cabling. Lifting tiles and working in the plenum creates hazards that are easy to underestimate: open holes in walkways, dense cable bundles, sharp tile edges, poor lighting, and pressurized airflow that can carry dust. Removing too many tiles at once can also affect cooling for nearby racks.

Some spaces in a data center may meet OSHA's definition of a confined space, and some may be permit-required confined spaces under 29 CFR 1910.146. Whether a given space qualifies depends on its size, access and hazards, so staff need to recognize the signs and follow the facility's program. This awareness course covers raised-floor hazards, safe under-floor work, confined space permit basics, and good airflow and cable management.

## Safety Scope & Limits

### Applicable standards and regulations

- OSHA 29 CFR 1910.146 (permit-required confined spaces)

### What this course does

- Builds awareness of raised-floor hazards and helps you recognize spaces that may be confined spaces or permit-required confined spaces.

### What it does not do

- Is awareness-level only. It does not provide the entrant, attendant, or entry supervisor training required by 1910.146(g).
- Does not authorize permit-space entry. Entry requires your employer's permit program, trained personnel, and site-specific procedures.

An online course alone does not make anyone qualified for hazardous work. Under OSHA rules, the employer decides who is qualified, based on training plus demonstrated skill on the specific equipment and tasks at your site. Referencing a standard does not mean the organization that publishes it has reviewed or endorsed this course.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the space under a raised floor a confined space?

It depends on the space. OSHA defines a confined space as large enough to enter, with limited means of entry or exit, and not designed for continuous occupancy. A deep plenum may meet that definition. Your facility's confined space evaluation decides how each space is classified.

### How many floor tiles can be removed at once?

There is no universal number. Each open tile creates a fall hazard and changes airflow under the floor, which can cause hot spots. Facilities usually set limits, barricade open tiles and replace them as soon as work is finished. The course covers safe practices for this work.

### Who should take this course?

Facilities technicians, cabling crews and IT staff who lift tiles, run cable under the floor or enter other tight spaces in a data center.
