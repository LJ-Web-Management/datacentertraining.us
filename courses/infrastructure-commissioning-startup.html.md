## Why This Matters in a Data Center

Commissioning is where a data center proves it works as designed before live load arrives. Problems found at this stage, such as wrong breaker settings, controls that do not sequence correctly or cooling that cannot hold temperature at full load, are far cheaper to fix than after customers move in.

Data center commissioning is usually done in levels: factory witness testing, delivery and installation checks, startup, functional performance testing of individual systems and finally integrated systems testing (IST), which fails utility power and other components while load banks simulate IT load. ASHRAE Guideline 0 describes the general commissioning process. This program covers pre-commissioning planning, electrical, mechanical and controls testing, performance baselines and operational readiness.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is integrated systems testing (IST)?

IST is the final level of commissioning. It tests how power, cooling, controls and fire systems work together under realistic conditions, such as a utility failure with load banks simulating full IT load. It shows whether the facility actually rides through failures as designed.

### What are commissioning levels 1 through 5?

They are a common way to structure data center commissioning: Level 1 factory testing, Level 2 delivery and installation inspection, Level 3 startup, Level 4 functional testing of each system and Level 5 integrated systems testing. Exact definitions vary by project.

### Is commissioning only for new builds?

No. Expansions, major equipment replacements and control upgrades also need commissioning, and recommissioning an existing facility can find problems that have developed over time.

### What is commissioning and startup?

Startup is the first energization and operation of a piece of equipment, usually by its manufacturer. Commissioning is the broader process that verifies each system, and all systems working together, meet the design intent before the facility carries live load. In data centers it is usually organized in five levels, ending with integrated systems testing.
