OSHA Electrical Safety-Related Work Practices for Data Center Technicians

OSHA-aligned electrical safety-related work practices tailored to data center technicians.

2 hrs · self-paced online Power & Electrical NFPA 70E

This course applies OSHA's electrical safety-related work practices (29 CFR 1910 Subpart S) specifically to the data center technician role: the equipment, access patterns, and daily tasks that put technicians near energized systems more often than in a typical facility.

You'll leave with approach boundaries and documentation practices that keep your electrical safety program audit-ready.

What You'll Learn

  • Apply OSHA electrical safety-related work practices to daily data center tasks
  • Identify the data center-specific tasks that carry elevated electrical risk
  • Follow correct approach boundaries for qualified and unqualified persons
  • Document electrical safety compliance correctly

Course Modules

OSHA Work Practice Requirements

What 29 CFR 1910 Subpart S actually requires.

Data Center-Specific Risk Tasks

The daily tasks that put technicians near energized equipment.

Approach Boundaries

Qualified vs. unqualified person boundaries, applied correctly.

Documentation & Compliance

Keeping electrical safety compliance records audit-ready.

Who This Course Is For

Audience: Data center technicians who work on or near energized electrical equipment.
