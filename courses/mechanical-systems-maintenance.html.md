## Why This Matters in a Data Center

Chillers, pumps, cooling towers and piping keep a data center's heat moving out of the building. They run continuously, often for years without a full shutdown, so maintenance has to be planned carefully and done without losing cooling redundancy.

Water quality and equipment condition cause many mechanical failures. Poor water treatment leads to scale, corrosion and biological growth, including Legionella risk in cooling towers, which ASHRAE Standard 188 addresses. Vibration analysis can find bearing wear, misalignment and imbalance in pumps and fans long before they fail. This course covers piping and water treatment, chiller operation and maintenance, vibration analysis and lifecycle planning with vendors.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is water treatment important for data center cooling?

Untreated water causes scale that reduces heat transfer, corrosion that damages pipes and equipment, and biological growth that fouls systems and can create health risks. A water treatment program keeps chillers and towers efficient and reliable.

### What can vibration analysis detect?

Vibration analysis can detect imbalance, misalignment, looseness, bearing wear and some electrical faults in rotating equipment such as pumps, fans and compressors, often months before failure.

### How do you maintain a chiller without losing cooling?

By planning around redundancy: confirming the remaining chillers can carry the load, following a method of procedure, scheduling during cooler conditions or lower load when possible, and having a backout plan. The course covers this within chiller operations and lifecycle planning.
