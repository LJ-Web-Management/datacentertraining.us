## Why This Matters in a Data Center

Data center infrastructure management (DCIM) software is meant to give one view of assets, power, cooling and space. In practice, many deployments fall short because asset data is incomplete, integrations with power and cooling systems were never finished or the tool was chosen for features the team does not use.

A good DCIM program starts with strategy: what questions it needs to answer, where the data will come from and who will keep it accurate. With that in place, DCIM can show available capacity by rack, model the effect of new deployments on power and cooling, and support planning and audits. This vendor-neutral course covers DCIM architecture, asset inventory, power and thermal modeling, reporting and analytics, and how to evaluate and select a platform.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does DCIM stand for?

Data center infrastructure management. It refers to software that tracks physical assets and monitors or models power, cooling and space so operators and planners can manage capacity and changes.

### Does this course teach a specific DCIM product?

No. It is platform-agnostic and focuses on strategy, data and evaluation criteria so you can choose, implement or improve any DCIM platform.

### What is the most common reason DCIM projects struggle?

Data quality. If the asset inventory is not complete and kept current, capacity and modeling results cannot be trusted. The Asset Inventory & Tracking module covers how to build and maintain accurate data.
