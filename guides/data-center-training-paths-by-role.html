Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to take the courses in this order?

No. Every course is self-paced and sold individually. The order is a suggestion that puts safety and fundamentals before advanced topics.

Does finishing a path give me a certification?

No. Each course awards a certificate of completion. A path is not a professional certification, and no course carries IACET CEUs or PDHs.

Is there a discount for a whole path?

Track bundles group the courses for a role at a lower price than buying them separately, and volume pricing applies from 2 seats.

Safety courses provide the knowledge portion of training. They do not by themselves qualify anyone for energized electrical work, confined space entry, refrigerant handling, or other hazardous tasks; your employer decides who is qualified. Prices are per seat and current as of the date above.
