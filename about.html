About Data Center Training

We publish online, self-paced training for the people who build, run, and protect data centers, plus free guides, career resources, quizzes, and calculators for anyone curious about the industry.

Who We Are

Data Center Training (datacentertraining.us) is a specialty training division of HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC, which is a subsidiary of Industrial Certified Training, LLC. HAZWOPER OSHA Training delivers online safety and compliance training and is an IACET Accredited Provider.

That accreditation belongs to the company. The data center courses on this site do not carry IACET CEUs or any other continuing education credit. Learners receive a certificate of completion for each course they finish. Our certificates and accreditation page explains exactly what a certificate does and does not mean.

What We Do

  • Online courses. A catalog of self-paced courses on data center power, cooling, safety, security, operations, design, and monitoring, sold individually or in role-based bundles, for individuals and for teams.
  • Free guides. The Knowledge Center explains how data centers work, from power and cooling to Tier levels and standards.
  • Career resources. The Careers hub covers job roles, entry-level jobs, pay data, certifications, and interviews.
  • Training guides. Training by topic explains what NFPA 70E, arc flash, lockout/tagout, and equipment training cover and who needs it.
  • Quizzes and tools. Quizzes to test what you know, and calculators for PUE, uptime, cooling load, and training plans.

We sell training, and our free pages recommend our courses where they fit. We say so plainly, and we try to make every guide useful whether or not you buy anything.

Editorial Standards

Our guides are written for working professionals and people entering the field, so accuracy matters more than volume. Every guide and career page follows these rules:

  1. Primary sources first. Facts about regulations and standards come from the issuing body (OSHA, NFPA, EPA, TIA, ASHRAE, Uptime Institute, ISO). Facts about energy and the industry come from government and research sources such as the U.S. Department of Energy and national laboratories. Pages with external facts list their sources at the end.
  2. Pay data from the Bureau of Labor Statistics only. When we quote pay, we use U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics figures, name the occupation and the year, and link to the source. The BLS has no "data center technician" occupation, so we show comparable occupations and say so. We do not publish pay figures from job boards or estimate any employer's pay.
  3. No invented numbers. We do not publish made-up statistics, job counts, rankings, or testimonials. If we cannot verify a claim, we leave it out or describe it in general terms.
  4. Companies and locations are described from official sources. When we mention an employer, a project, or a hiring program, we rely on the company's own pages or announcements, or on government and major news sources, and link to them. Data Center Training is not affiliated with any employer named on this site, and no employer requires or endorses our courses.
  5. Honest credentials. Our courses award certificates of completion. We never call them certifications, licenses, or qualifications, and we never claim a standards body has approved them. Online training supports, but does not replace, hands-on practice, site-specific procedures, and the qualification your employer is responsible for.
  6. Dated and updated. Each guide shows when it was last updated. Standards and regulations change, so always confirm the current edition or rule with its publisher before relying on it for compliance.

Corrections and Feedback

If you find an error, an outdated rule, or a broken source link, email info@hazwoper-osha.com with the page address and what needs to change. We review every report and correct confirmed errors promptly.
