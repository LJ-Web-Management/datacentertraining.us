## Common Questions

**What's the difference between Comprehensive Programs and Modular Specializations?**

Comprehensive Programs are multi-module courses (8 to 16 hours) covering a full discipline in depth, like data center design or operations management. Modular Specializations are focused 1-8 hour courses on a single skill, like UPS load testing or fiber termination. Many teams combine one comprehensive program with several modular courses for their role, which is exactly how our bundles are put together.

**Can I choose only the courses my team needs?**

Yes. Every course is sold individually, so you can enroll each person in exactly the courses their role requires, without paying for training your team doesn't need.

**Do you offer volume pricing for large teams?**

Yes. Seat-count discounts apply automatically at checkout on a per-course or per-bundle basis, from 2 seats up through 1,000+.

**Do learners get a certificate?**

Yes. Every course awards a certificate of completion from HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC as soon as the learner finishes, with a unique certificate number you can [verify online](https://datacentertraining.us/certifications.html#verify). It documents the training. It is not a license or a certification from a standards body, and these courses do not carry IACET CEUs or PDHs.

**Is there a deadline to finish a course?**

No. Courses are self-paced with no fixed completion deadline, so learners can start, stop, and pick back up on their own schedule.

**Can I take courses on my phone or tablet?**

Yes. Courses run in any modern web browser, so you can start on a desktop and continue on a phone or tablet without installing a separate app.

**Can I track my team's progress?**

Yes. A training manager can enroll learners, assign or change them from the dashboard after purchase, and track each learner's progress, grades, and completions in real time on HAZWOPER OSHA Training's learning management system.

**Can my company pay by purchase order?**

Yes. Pay by credit or debit card at checkout, or use a purchase order number for a corporate account. For purchase orders, bundles, or invoice billing, call [1-866-429-6742](tel:18664296742) or email [info@hazwoper-osha.com](mailto:info@hazwoper-osha.com).

**What if you don't offer the course I need?**

Send us a [course request](https://datacentertraining.us/course-request.html). It opens an email to our sales team with the topic, audience, and timeline you need, and the team will let you know whether it can offer it.
