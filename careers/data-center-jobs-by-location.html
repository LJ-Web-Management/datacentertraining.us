Short answer: To find data center jobs near you, start with where operators actually build. Northern Virginia, Dallas-Fort Worth, Phoenix, Atlanta, Chicago, and Central Ohio are established hubs, while new AI campuses are hiring in places like Abilene, El Paso, Richland Parish, and southeast Wisconsin. Then search each operator's careers site by city for technician and facilities titles.

Key takeaways

  • Big campuses are often in small towns outside the metro, so search by county and nearby cities, not just the big city name.
  • New campuses hire construction trades first (electricians, pipefitters, commissioning), then a much smaller permanent operations team.
  • Google, Meta, and Microsoft publish lists of their data center locations, which is the fastest way to see who operates near you.

How do I find data center jobs near me?

Find out which operators have facilities near you, then search their careers sites with a location filter and their own job titles. "Data center technician jobs near me" searches on general job boards miss many facilities roles, because each company names them differently.

  1. Map the operators near you

    Google's data center locations page, Meta's U.S. data center list, and Microsoft's community pages show where each company operates or is building. Colocation companies list their sites on their own websites.

  2. Search careers sites by location and title

    Use each company's own titles: Data Center Engineering Operations Technician at AWS, Data Center Facilities Technician at Google, Critical Environment Technician at Microsoft, Critical Facilities Technician at many colocation firms. Our hyperscale data center jobs guide lists them.

  3. Widen the radius

    Campuses often sit 20 to 60 miles from the metro they are named after. Set alerts for nearby counties and small towns, not only the big city.

  4. Include contractors and the trades

    Construction, commissioning, and much of the security and maintenance work is done by contractors. Electrical and mechanical contractors, union halls, and staffing firms hire for these sites; ask them which local projects they staff.

  5. Use public workforce resources

    Your local American Job Center (find one through CareerOneStop, sponsored by the U.S. Department of Labor) and the Apprenticeship.gov job finder list openings and apprenticeships near you. Community colleges near big campuses often run employer-backed programs.

New to the industry? Read entry-level data center jobs first, so you know which titles to search for.

Virginia and the Mid-Atlantic

Virginia is the country's densest data center market, and the region around it is drawing new campuses.

Northern Virginia and Ashburn

Loudoun County, nicknamed "Data Center Alley" by its own economic development director, has the highest concentration of data centers in the U.S., according to The Christian Science Monitor. It grew around an early internet exchange point (MAE-East) plus available power, water, and fiber. Google lists data centers in Loudoun and Prince William counties, and AWS announced in 2023 plans to invest $35 billion by 2040 in multiple Virginia data center campuses. Recent official postings include an Oracle Data Center Technician in Ashburn, an Equinix logistics role in Ashburn and customer operations technician in Culpeper, and AWS "Data Center Engineering Operations Technician, Northern Virginia" roles.

Roles in demand: IT data center technicians, critical facilities technicians, colocation customer operations, security, and commissioning. Local training: Germanna Community College hosts the AWS Information Infrastructure Pre-Apprenticeship (i2PA), a paid four-week program.

Richmond, Virginia

Meta's Henrico data center, east of Richmond, broke ground in 2017; Meta reports about 1,500 skilled trade workers on site at peak construction and 200-plus operational jobs supported. Google lists Chesterfield County, south of Richmond, as a location in development. Search Henrico and Chesterfield as well as Richmond.

Maryland

Maryland's best-known project is the planned Quantum Frederick campus in Frederick County. A 2023 study commissioned by the Maryland Tech Council and prepared by Sage Policy Group projected about 1,700 jobs at completion. Large campus plans can change, so confirm the current status before planning a move. Maryland job seekers also commute to Northern Virginia sites.

New Jersey

New Jersey's data center jobs center on colocation sites serving the New York financial and network market. Equinix, for example, has posted Data Center Critical Facilities Lead and Customer Operations Technician roles in Secaucus. Search colocation companies' careers sites with a New Jersey filter.

Texas data center jobs

Texas has both long-running hubs and some of the largest AI campuses under construction, so "data center jobs in Texas" covers everything from technician roles in the metros to construction trades in rural counties.

Dallas-Fort Worth

Meta's Fort Worth data center broke ground in 2015; Meta reports about 1,200 trade workers at peak and 200-plus operational jobs supported. Google lists data centers in Midlothian and Red Oak, south of Dallas, and Oracle has posted a commissioning engineer role for the Dallas-Fort Worth area. Roles in demand: facilities technicians, IT technicians, commissioning, and controls.

San Antonio

Microsoft says it operates datacenters in Bexar and Medina counties in the Greater San Antonio area. Its community page highlights Datacenter Academy scholarships at Northwest Vista College. Roles in demand: Microsoft's two core titles, Data Center Technician and Critical Environment Technician, plus electrical and mechanical trades.

Austin, Central Texas, and Houston

Meta's Temple data center, north of Austin, broke ground in 2022 with about 1,200 trade workers at peak and roughly 100 operational jobs. Milam County, northeast of Austin, is one of the Stargate AI sites announced in September 2025 by OpenAI, Oracle, and SoftBank. Houston job seekers should run the same searches with a Houston filter and include colocation and enterprise employers.

Abilene and Shackelford County

Abilene is home to the flagship Stargate AI campus. SoftBank's September 2025 announcement said the Abilene site was already running on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure. Trade press reports the campus was developed by Crusoe and leased to Oracle and OpenAI, with eight buildings and about 1.2 GW of planned power; when Crusoe topped out the final building in November 2025, it thanked 7,000 electricians, construction workers, and skilled tradespeople. Nearby Shackelford County is another announced Stargate site. Oracle has posted Senior Data Center Support Services Technician roles in Abilene, including military pathway roles, and Vantage has posted a Facility Technician role in Shackelford County. See AI data center jobs for what these campuses need.

El Paso and southern New Mexico

Meta's El Paso data center broke ground in 2025. Meta lists a $10 billion-plus investment, more than 4,000 skilled trade workers on site at peak construction, and 300-plus operational jobs. Workforce Solutions Borderplex is a community partner in Meta's America's Workforce Academy, which launched its Texas pilot in 2026. Just across the state line, Doña Ana County, New Mexico, is a Stargate site, and Oracle has posted facilities operations and support services roles in Santa Teresa and Anthony, New Mexico.

Southwest and Mountain West

Phoenix and Arizona

Microsoft says it operates datacenters in El Mirage and Goodyear in the West Valley. Google lists a Mesa data center, Meta's Mesa site (broke ground 2021) reports about 2,000 trade workers at peak and 200-plus operational jobs, and Oracle and AWS have posted technician roles in Phoenix. Local training: the West Valley Microsoft Datacenter Academy runs at Estrella Mountain and Glendale community colleges.

Cheyenne, Wyoming

Microsoft operates a datacenter in Cheyenne, and its community page highlights a partnership with Laramie County Community College. Meta's Cheyenne data center broke ground in 2024 with more than 1,000 trade workers at peak and roughly 100 operational jobs planned. CoreWeave has posted Data Center Technician and Data Center Manager roles in Cheyenne. For a small city, that is an unusual concentration of employers.

Southeast

Atlanta and Georgia

Google lists a data center in Douglas County, west of Atlanta. Meta's Stanton Springs site, east of the city, broke ground in 2018 and supports 200-plus operational jobs. Microsoft says it is building datacenters in Douglasville, Palmetto, and East Point, and AWS announced in January 2025 that it will invest $11 billion in Douglas and Butts counties, with at least 550 new jobs expected. Microsoft also highlights a Datacenter Academy expansion in Georgia. Roles in demand: technicians, critical facilities, security, and construction trades.

West Memphis, Arkansas

Google broke ground in October 2025 on a $4 billion data center campus in West Memphis. Local coverage quoted Google saying the project will provide thousands of construction jobs and eventually employ hundreds. Google lists West Memphis as in development and has posted Data Center Facilities Technician roles there. Commuters from the Memphis, Tennessee, area should include West Memphis in their searches.

Louisiana

Meta announced in December 2024 that it is building its largest data center to date in Richland Parish in northeast Louisiana. Meta's location page lists more than 7,500 skilled trade workers on site at peak construction and about 1,000 operational jobs supported once completed. Louisiana is a 2026 pilot state for Meta's America's Workforce Academy, with the Richland Parish Chamber of Commerce as a community partner. Roles in demand: electricians, pipefitters, and other trades now; facilities and technician roles as buildings come online.

Midwest

Chicago and Illinois

Meta's DeKalb data center, west of Chicago, broke ground in 2020 and supports 200-plus operational jobs. Equinix has posted critical facilities supervisor roles in Elk Grove Village, and CoreWeave has posted Data Center Technician roles in Elk Grove and Volo. Roles in demand: critical facilities engineers and technicians, IT technicians.

Columbus and Central Ohio

Central Ohio is one of the busiest new markets. Google lists data centers in Columbus, New Albany, and Lancaster. Meta's New Albany site supports 300-plus operational jobs. Microsoft says it is building datacenters in New Albany, Heath, and Hebron. AWS, which already ran campuses in two Central Ohio counties, announced in 2023 a $7.8 billion expansion by the end of 2029, with state officials projecting 230 direct jobs and about 1,000 support jobs. Ohio is also a 2026 pilot state for Meta's America's Workforce Academy. Elsewhere in Ohio, Meta is building in Bowling Green and SoftBank is developing a Stargate site in Lordstown.

Iowa: Cedar Rapids, Des Moines, and Council Bluffs

Google lists Council Bluffs as an operating site and Cedar Rapids as in development. Microsoft operates a datacenter in West Des Moines. Meta's Altoona site, east of Des Moines, broke ground in 2013 and supports 400-plus operational jobs. Roles in demand: technicians and facilities staff at established sites, construction and commissioning at Cedar Rapids.

Indiana: Fort Wayne and beyond

Indiana announced in April 2024 that Google is building a $2 billion data center in Fort Wayne, expected to support up to 200 jobs in facilities, data center technician, and support services roles such as security, catering, and grounds keeping. Google is also bringing its Skilled Trades and Readiness (STAR) program to Fort Wayne with Ivy Tech Community College. Meta is building in Lebanon (a $10 billion-plus site with roughly 300 operational jobs planned) and Jeffersonville, and Indiana is a 2026 America's Workforce Academy pilot state.

Southeast Wisconsin: Mount Pleasant and Port Washington

Microsoft's AI datacenter in Mount Pleasant employed more than 3,000 construction workers at daily peak, according to Microsoft, which expects around 500 full-time employees, growing to around 800. Students at Gateway Technical College can train through Wisconsin's first Microsoft Datacenter Academy. In Port Washington, north of Milwaukee, Vantage, Oracle, and OpenAI announced the Lighthouse campus in October 2025, with more than 4,000 skilled construction jobs expected (mostly union) and more than 1,000 long-term jobs once complete. Meta is also building in Beaver Dam.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find data center jobs near me?

Start by finding which operators have facilities near you, using the location pages that Google, Meta, and Microsoft publish. Then search each operator's careers site with a location filter and the company's own titles, such as Data Center Technician, Critical Facilities Technician, or Critical Environment Technician. Add contractors, staffing firms, and your local American Job Center to the search.

Where are the most data center jobs in the United States?

There is no single official count of data center jobs by city. Northern Virginia, especially Loudoun County and Ashburn, has the highest concentration of data centers in the country, and large clusters also exist around Dallas-Fort Worth, Phoenix, Atlanta, Chicago, and Central Ohio. New AI campuses are adding jobs in places such as Abilene, El Paso, Richland Parish, and southeast Wisconsin.

Are there data center jobs in Texas?

Yes. Meta operates or is building data centers in Fort Worth, Temple, and El Paso, Google lists Midlothian and Red Oak near Dallas, Microsoft operates datacenters in the San Antonio area, and the Stargate AI campus in Abilene is running on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure. Oracle and Vantage have posted facilities and technician roles in Shackelford County.

What data center jobs are available in Abilene, Texas?

Abilene is home to the flagship Stargate AI campus, developed by Crusoe and leased to Oracle and OpenAI. Oracle has posted data center support services technician roles in Abilene, including military pathway roles. During construction, the campus also employed thousands of electricians and other skilled trades workers through contractors.

Several community colleges near large campuses also run employer-backed programs, such as the Microsoft Datacenter Academy in Arizona and Wisconsin and AWS-funded programs in Virginia.
