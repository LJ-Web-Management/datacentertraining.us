Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how datacentertraining.us (the "Site") collects, uses, and shares personal information, and the choices you have. The Site is operated by HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), a subsidiary of Industrial Certified Training, LLC. Data Center Training is a specialty training division of HAZWOPER OSHA Training.

This policy covers the Site only. When you take a course, your learner account, course progress, assessments, and certificates are handled on HAZWOPER OSHA Training's learning platform under the HAZWOPER OSHA Training Privacy Policy.

Summary

  • We do not sell your personal information for money.
  • Analytics, session recording, and live chat are off unless you turn them on.
  • We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal as an opt-out.
  • You can change your choices at any time with the Your Privacy Choices link in the footer of every page.

Your Privacy Choices

When you first visit, the Site asks whether to turn on analytics and session recording, and live chat. Nothing in those categories loads until you agree. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time:

Opt-out of sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information for money. Some state laws may treat analytics tools as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. Because those tools are off unless you opt in, choosing "Reject all" (or never opting in) opts you out. You can also email or call us to opt out.

Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends a GPC signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of sale and sharing, and analytics and session recording stay off for that browser. Browsers' older "Do Not Track" setting has no agreed standard, so we rely on GPC instead.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, and other states with consumer privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you and how we use and disclose it, and get a copy of it.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Delete personal information we collected from you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

How to make a request: email info@hazwoper-osha.com with the subject "Privacy request," or call 1-866-429-6742. We will verify your request by matching information you give us with information we hold, and we respond within the time required by law. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof of the agent's authority and verify your identity directly. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision with the subject "Privacy appeal."

California "Shine the Light." We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes.

Children

The Site and our courses are intended for adults in the workforce. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including providing training records and certificates, meeting tax, accounting, and legal requirements, and resolving disputes. Analytics and chat providers keep data according to their own retention settings.

Security

The Site is served only over HTTPS, and payments are handled by a PCI DSS-compliant processor. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
