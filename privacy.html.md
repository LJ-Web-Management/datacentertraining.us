## Summary

- We do not sell your personal information for money.
- We use analytics, session recording, and live chat for every visitor. You can **turn them off at any time**.
- We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal as an opt-out.
- You can change your choices at any time with the [Your Privacy Choices](https://datacentertraining.us/privacy.html#choices) link in the footer of every page.

## Your Privacy Choices

Analytics and session recording, and live chat, are on by default. You can turn either one off for your browser at any time, and turn it back on later:

**Opt-out of sale or sharing.** We do not sell personal information for money. Some state laws may treat analytics tools as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. To opt out, open Your Privacy Choices and choose "Turn all off," or uncheck analytics and save. Your choice is stored in your browser, so repeat it on each browser or device you use, or email or call us to opt out.

**Global Privacy Control.** If your browser sends a GPC signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of sale and sharing, and analytics and session recording stay off for that browser. Browsers' older "Do Not Track" setting has no agreed standard, so we rely on GPC instead.

## Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, and other states with consumer privacy laws, you may have the right to:

- Delete personal information we collected from you, subject to legal exceptions.
- Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising.
- Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

**How to make a request:** email [info@hazwoper-osha.com](mailto:info@hazwoper-osha.com?subject=Privacy%20request) with the subject "Privacy request," or call 1-866-429-6742.
