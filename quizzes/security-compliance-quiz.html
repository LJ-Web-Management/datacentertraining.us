Data Center Security & Compliance Quiz

Ten questions on how data centers control who gets in and how auditors check it, from mantraps to SOC 2 and PCI DSS. Each answer comes with a short explanation.

  1. Question 1Data center physical security is built in layers. Which order runs from the outside in?
    Show answer

    Answer: perimeter, building, data hall, cabinet. Each layer delays and detects an intruder and needs its own authorization, so one failed control does not expose the equipment. Cameras, guards, and alarms back up every layer. Read the security and compliance guide.

  2. Question 2What is a mantrap (access control vestibule) designed to prevent?
    Show answer

    Answer: tailgating and piggybacking. A mantrap has two interlocked doors, and the second will not open until the first has closed. Many add a second factor such as biometrics, or sensors that detect more than one person inside. Access Control & Physical Security covers entry design.

  3. Question 3A vendor technician arrives to service a CRAH but is not on the access list. What is the correct practice?
    Show answer

    Answer: verify, log, badge, and escort. Visitors are checked against a scheduled request, identified, logged, badged, and escorted at all times inside controlled areas. Sharing badges or codes breaks the audit trail that SOC 2, ISO/IEC 27001, and PCI DSS assessors look for. Data Center Physical Security and Access Control Training covers visitor handling.

  4. Question 4Which list matches the five AICPA Trust Services Criteria used in SOC 2 reports?
    Show answer

    Answer: security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy. Security is included in every SOC 2 report, and the other criteria are added when they fit the service; data centers often include availability. Option b lists functions from the NIST Cybersecurity Framework. Security & Compliance for Data Centers covers SOC 2 audits.

  5. Question 5Which statement about ISO/IEC 27001 is correct?
    Show answer

    Answer: it is the international ISMS standard. Accredited certification bodies audit and certify organizations against ISO/IEC 27001. Its Annex A controls include physical security, such as secure perimeters, entry controls, equipment protection, and secure disposal. Security & Compliance for Data Centers compares the frameworks.

  6. Question 6In PCI DSS, what does Requirement 9 cover?
    Show answer

    Answer: restricting physical access to cardholder data. Requirement 9 covers facility entry controls, visitor handling, and the protection and destruction of media. In a colocation facility, the provider often covers part of it for customers, which is documented in a responsibility matrix. Read the security and compliance guide.

  7. Question 7Which of these is a HIPAA Security Rule physical safeguard?
    Show answer

    Answer: facility access, workstation, and device and media controls. Physical safeguards are listed in 45 CFR 164.310. Risk analysis and training are administrative safeguards, and user IDs, audit logs, and transmission security are technical safeguards. Security & Compliance for Data Centers covers HIPAA requirements.

  8. Question 8Why do data centers run periodic access reviews?
    Show answer

    Answer: to remove access that is no longer needed. Reviews compare access lists against HR records and customer authorizations, and access for people who are no longer authorized is a classic audit finding. Terminations should be removed right away, not at the next review. Data Center Physical Security and Access Control Training covers access governance.

  9. Question 9A failed hard drive held customer data. What is the accepted way to handle it?
    Show answer

    Answer: chain of custody, then sanitize or destroy. NIST SP 800-88 describes clear, purge, and destroy methods, and degaussing works only on magnetic media, not SSDs. Many customers require that drives never leave the site intact. Security & Compliance for Data Centers covers media handling.

  10. Question 10When does EPA's SPCC rule (40 CFR Part 112) generally apply to a data center?
    Show answer

    Answer: above 1,320 gallons aboveground, with a path to water. Bulk diesel tanks, generator day tanks, and oil-filled transformers of 55 gallons or more count toward capacity. The Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure plan covers containment, inspections, training, and spill response. Facility Environmental Compliance covers SPCC plans.
