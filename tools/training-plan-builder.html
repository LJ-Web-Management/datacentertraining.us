    How the plan is built

    Every role on a data center floor needs a different mix of training. The builder starts with the core courses for the role you pick, ordered for your experience: fundamentals first if you are new, advanced courses first if you already do the work. It adds the top courses for any extra topics you choose, then fills your time budget in priority order.

    The plan is a starting point. Employers decide who is qualified for which duties, and some tasks also require hands-on training, site-specific procedures, or licenses that no online course replaces. Each course awards a certificate of completion from HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC, an IACET Accredited Provider. See what the certificate means.

    Frequently asked questions

    How does the training plan builder choose courses?

    It starts with the core courses for the role you pick, ordered for your experience level, adds the top courses for any extra topics you select, and then fills your time budget in priority order. You can uncheck any course, and the hours and prices update.

    Can I buy the plan for a whole team?

    Yes. Enter the number of seats and the total includes the seat-count volume discount. You can enroll through checkout, or email the plan to us and we will set up learner accounts and send an invoice or quote, including purchase-order billing.

    Can I enroll in all the courses in my plan at once?

    Yes. Select Enroll in All Courses and checkout lists every course in your plan at its regular per-seat price, so you pay for them together in one order. Orders for 2 or more seats get the seat-count volume discount.

    Prefer to Talk It Through?

    Tell us about your team and we will put together a plan, a quote, and purchase-order billing if you need it.
