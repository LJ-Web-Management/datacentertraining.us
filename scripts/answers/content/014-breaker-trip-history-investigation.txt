## The direct answer
Repeated breaker trips should be treated as evidence to investigate, not as a nuisance to reset; preserve event data and have qualified staff assess the cause.

## Why this matters
Repeatedly restoring a circuit without identifying the cause can expose equipment and workers to recurring fault conditions.

## Practical review points
- Capture trip time and affected load
- Correlate with recent changes and alarms
- Prevent unapproved repeated resets
- Route findings to the electrical authority

## Example from operations
A circuit trips twice during a shift after a rack addition. The operator records timing and the change ticket and escalates rather than attempting another reset.

## Frequently asked question
### What information helps investigate a breaker trip?
The circuit identity, event time, load context, related alarms, recent work, and actions already taken.
