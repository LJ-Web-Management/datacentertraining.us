## The direct answer
Trend retention should reflect troubleshooting, maintenance, compliance, customer, and capacity-planning needs, with ownership and time synchronization defined by policy.

## Why this matters
Short retention can erase evidence before an incident is reviewed; excessive retention without governance can create cost and access issues.

## Practical review points
- List decisions that require historical data
- Set retention by data class and purpose
- Confirm access and backup ownership
- Test retrieval before a real incident

## Example from operations
A cooling investigation begins after a monthly report flags a drift. The team discovers the detailed trend was overwritten, prompting a retention review.

## Frequently asked question
### Is there one mandatory retention period for all facility trends?
Not universally. Requirements depend on applicable rules, contracts, policy, and operational purpose.
