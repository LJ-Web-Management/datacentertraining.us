## The direct answer
Rack inlet targets should come from the facility's approved environmental policy and equipment requirements, then be monitored at representative locations rather than inferred from room air.

## Why this matters
A target chosen without equipment and location context can conceal localized overheating or waste cooling capacity.

## Practical review points
- Use current equipment and site criteria
- Measure at representative rack inlets
- Review both high and low excursions
- Coordinate target changes with engineering and operations

## Example from operations
A proposal raises a room setpoint to save energy. The manager asks for rack-level evidence and an agreed monitoring window before approving the change.

## Frequently asked question
### Is there one universal temperature target for every data center rack?
No. Use the applicable equipment guidance and facility limits for the actual environment.
