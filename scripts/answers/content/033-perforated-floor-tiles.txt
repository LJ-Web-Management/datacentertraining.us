## The direct answer
Floor tile selection should follow the cooling design and measured rack demand; tile placement and open area should not be changed informally to solve a local temperature issue.

## Why this matters
Unplanned tile changes can divert supply air from other racks and create new hot spots.

## Practical review points
- Check the approved airflow plan
- Review rack inlet data before moving tiles
- Coordinate changes with facilities control owner
- Measure again after the approved adjustment

## Example from operations
A team moves a high-flow tile toward one rack without recording the original pattern. A later survey finds a neighboring rack warmer, so the layout is returned to engineering review.

## Frequently asked question
### Does a higher-open-area tile always improve cooling?
No. It changes distribution and can reduce supply to nearby locations if not evaluated as part of the system.
