## The direct answer
A maintainability review asks whether planned work can isolate the intended component while required loads continue within the design's documented limits.

## Why this matters
A design can include spare equipment yet still require disruptive work if isolation boundaries or procedures are incomplete.

## Practical review points
- Identify the maintenance task and boundary
- Trace what remains in service
- Check controls and utility dependencies
- Record assumptions requiring test or procedure validation

## Example from operations
A pump is described as maintainable but its isolation plan also removes a shared sensor. The review captures the dependency before turnover.

## Frequently asked question
### How can a team verify maintainability before operation?
Review design and procedures together, then validate key assumptions through approved commissioning and operational reviews.
