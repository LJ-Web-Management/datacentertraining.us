## The direct answer
Cable records should connect endpoints, route, purpose, label, and revision so operations can trace service dependencies without relying on individual memory.

## Why this matters
Poor identification increases troubleshooting time and the chance of disturbing an unrelated or live connection.

## Practical review points
- Use a consistent label convention
- Record both endpoints and route changes
- Reconcile test results with installed links
- Transfer controlled records at handover

## Example from operations
A link issue is reported in a new hall, but the rack label does not match the turnover sheet. The project team resolves the mapping before accepting the cabling package.

## Frequently asked question
### Is labeling one end of a cable enough?
No. Traceability requires a method to identify both endpoints and the relationship between them.
