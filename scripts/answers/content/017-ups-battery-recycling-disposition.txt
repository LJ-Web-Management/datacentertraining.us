## The direct answer
Battery disposition should follow the chemistry-specific handling, storage, transport, and recycling requirements established by the site and applicable authorities.

## Why this matters
Uncontrolled storage or disposal can create chemical, fire, environmental, and recordkeeping problems.

## Practical review points
- Identify chemistry and condition before staging
- Use designated secure storage and labeling
- Coordinate approved recycler and transport
- Retain disposition documentation

## Example from operations
A battery replacement project leaves removed units awaiting pickup. The supervisor verifies segregation, labeling, and the approved collection route before the work area is released.

## Frequently asked question
### Can all UPS batteries be handled through the same recycling process?
No. Requirements and hazards vary by chemistry and condition; follow the applicable site and recycler instructions.
