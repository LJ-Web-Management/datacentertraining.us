## The direct answer
An emergency contact tree is useful only when current contacts, escalation order, alternates, and acknowledgement expectations are tested and updated.

## Why this matters
A stale list can delay decisions at the moment when staffing and utility conditions are changing.

## Practical review points
- Assign an owner and review date
- Include alternate roles and coverage windows
- Test acknowledgement and escalation
- Track failed contacts to closure

## Example from operations
A drill reaches an outdated number for the generator lead. The exercise records the gap and assigns an update before the next test.

## Frequently asked question
### Is maintaining a contact list enough?
No. Verify that contacts can be reached and know their role in the response.
