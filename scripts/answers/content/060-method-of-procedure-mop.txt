## The direct answer
A useful MOP identifies scope, prerequisites, roles, sequence, hold points, expected indications, stop conditions, communication, and recovery steps in language the approved team can use.

## Why this matters
A procedure that omits ownership or stop criteria can turn routine work into improvisation under pressure.

## Practical review points
- Name task lead and approver
- List dependencies and affected loads
- Define hold and stop conditions
- Review sequence with executing staff before the window

## Example from operations
A document says “switch to standby” without stating who verifies the result. The pre-job review returns it for clarification before work is authorized.

## Frequently asked question
### What is the most important part of a MOP?
A complete, reviewed sequence with clear ownership and stop conditions; no single checklist item makes an unsafe procedure acceptable.
