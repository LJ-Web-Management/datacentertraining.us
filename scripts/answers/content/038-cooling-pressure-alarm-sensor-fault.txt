## The direct answer
A pressure alarm should be compared with related points, unit mode, and recent work before diagnosing a process fault or sensor problem.

## Why this matters
An unverified pressure value can prompt inappropriate valve or pump adjustments.

## Practical review points
- Record the alarm and unit state
- Compare related supply and return readings
- Review sensor maintenance and recent changes
- Escalate any limit breach under site procedure

## Example from operations
A pressure alarm appears after maintenance while adjacent sensors remain stable. The technician reports the discrepancy and avoids changing the control sequence without approval.

## Frequently asked question
### What makes a pressure alarm more likely to be a sensor issue?
A mismatch with independent indicators may be a clue, but qualified review is needed to confirm the cause.
