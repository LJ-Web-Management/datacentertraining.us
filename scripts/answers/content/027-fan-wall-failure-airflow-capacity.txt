## The direct answer
After a fan-wall failure, teams should verify the remaining capacity and control response from approved monitoring and operating procedures before assuming redundancy is sufficient.

## Why this matters
A failed fan can change airflow distribution even if the overall unit remains online.

## Practical review points
- Identify failed fan and affected section
- Review total airflow and differential indicators
- Check alarms and redundancy status
- Escalate if conditions approach site thresholds

## Example from operations
A fan alarm appears in a multi-fan unit. Operators monitor relevant trends and notify the mechanical lead while holding new rack load additions.

## Frequently asked question
### Can a multi-fan unit always run safely with one fan offline?
Not automatically. Capacity depends on design, loading, controls, and environmental conditions.
