## The direct answer
A missing trend is an instrumentation or data-availability issue to document and escalate; staff should not infer normal conditions from the absence of a reading.

## Why this matters
A blank chart can conceal a sensor outage precisely when the team needs evidence to make a safe decision.

## Practical review points
- Identify point and gap duration
- Check related alarms and redundant measurements
- Record whether the gap affects a decision
- Track restoration and data quality review

## Example from operations
A chilled-water flow trend disappears during a maintenance window. The operator uses approved alternate indicators and notifies the controls owner rather than reading the blank as stable flow.

## Frequently asked question
### Does no alarm mean a missing trend is normal?
No. Absence of data is not evidence of a normal condition.
