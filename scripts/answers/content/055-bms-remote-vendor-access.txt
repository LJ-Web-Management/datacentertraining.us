## The direct answer
Remote vendor access should be approved, scoped, time-bounded, attributable to an individual, monitored, and closed after the support need ends.

## Why this matters
Shared or persistent access can expose control systems to unauthorized changes and make actions hard to trace.

## Practical review points
- Confirm sponsor and business purpose
- Use individual and least-privilege access
- Define session window and monitoring
- Revoke access and preserve logs after work

## Example from operations
A vendor requests a standing remote account to support a BAS. The facility and security owners define a time-limited support method with named accountability.

## Frequently asked question
### Should a vendor keep access between service calls for convenience?
No. Retain only access that is approved and necessary under the organization's security process.
