## The direct answer
Phase loading should be interpreted against equipment design limits, measurement context, and the facility's normal operating range; trend direction matters as much as one reading.

## Why this matters
Unreviewed imbalance can complicate capacity decisions and signal that loads are being added without coordinated planning.

## Practical review points
- Use comparable measurement points and time windows
- Compare trends with load changes
- Escalate readings outside approved limits
- Coordinate any redistribution with qualified staff

## Example from operations
A new server deployment shifts one phase's trend upward. The team compares the change with the rack and circuit plan before authorizing further additions.

## Frequently asked question
### Should a technician move loads to balance phases immediately?
No. Any redistribution must be engineered and authorized under site electrical procedures.
