## The direct answer
A maintenance window plan should identify affected teams, customer or tenant notices, decision authority, status checkpoints, and a contact path if scope or timing changes.

## Why this matters
Late or incomplete communication can leave dependent teams unprepared for an expected constraint or unexpected delay.

## Practical review points
- List all affected service and facility owners
- Specify start, hold, and end notifications
- Provide a change escalation contact
- Confirm acknowledgements for critical dependencies

## Example from operations
A cooling maintenance window shifts by two hours. The coordinator uses the contact list and approval path rather than relying on an informal chat message.

## Frequently asked question
### Who should receive a maintenance window notice?
Everyone whose work, service, access, or risk may be affected, as defined by the site's communication plan.
