## The direct answer
Fuel continuity planning should examine consumption assumptions, delivery access, supplier dependencies, contract terms, testing, and escalation triggers in the context of the site's resilience plan.

## Why this matters
On-site inventory does not guarantee replenishment during a regional disruption or blocked delivery route.

## Practical review points
- Document consumption and reserve assumptions
- Confirm supplier and after-hours contact
- Review access and delivery constraints
- Define escalation thresholds and decision owner

## Example from operations
A regional storm affects the normal delivery route. The facility plan identifies alternate contacts and the person who can authorize contingency actions.

## Frequently asked question
### Does a full tank guarantee generator fuel continuity?
No. The plan should also account for duration, replenishment, access, and supplier availability.
