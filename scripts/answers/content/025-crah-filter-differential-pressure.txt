## The direct answer
Filter differential pressure can help indicate loading, but teams should compare it with the manufacturer's criteria, airflow conditions, sensor reliability, and the unit's maintenance history.

## Why this matters
Replacing filters based on one unexplained value can waste resources or leave an airflow issue unresolved.

## Practical review points
- Confirm sensor and filter identification
- Compare trend with airflow and alarm data
- Use approved replacement criteria
- Document filter change and post-maintenance response

## Example from operations
A pressure alarm occurs shortly after a filter replacement. The technician checks installation direction and sensor tubing before ordering another filter.

## Frequently asked question
### Does high filter differential pressure always mean the filter must be replaced?
No. Confirm the measurement and use the equipment's approved criteria before deciding.
