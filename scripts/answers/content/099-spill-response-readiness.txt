## The direct answer
Spill readiness requires knowing the materials present, who is trained to respond, what protective equipment and reporting rules apply, and when the area must be isolated or escalated.

## Why this matters
An improvised cleanup can increase exposure or spread a release beyond the original area.

## Practical review points
- Identify materials and accessible safety information
- Post response contacts and limits
- Check kit condition and location
- Practice notification and waste handling route

## Example from operations
A small fluid release occurs in a mechanical space. The observer keeps others away and contacts the trained responder rather than selecting an unfamiliar cleanup material.

## Frequently asked question
### Should every employee clean up a small spill?
No. Follow site policy and respond only within assigned training and equipment limits.
