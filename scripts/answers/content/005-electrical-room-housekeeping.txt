## The direct answer
Electrical-room housekeeping means keeping access, work areas, equipment identification, and emergency routes clear under site rules; it is a routine condition review, not permission to work inside equipment.

## Why this matters
Clutter can delay access, hide damage, obstruct safe work, and complicate emergency response around energized equipment.

## Practical review points
- Keep designated access and egress clear
- Remove only items staff are authorized to handle
- Report leaks, dust buildup, or damaged enclosures
- Document recurring storage issues for the room owner

## Example from operations
A contractor leaves packaging beside a switchboard after delivery. The escort asks the contractor to remove it from the designated area and reports the repeated storage problem.

## Frequently asked question
### Can general facility staff inspect electrical equipment interiors?
No. Housekeeping observations from a safe position do not authorize opening enclosures or performing electrical work.
