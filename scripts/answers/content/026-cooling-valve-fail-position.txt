## The direct answer
A control valve's fail position should be understood as part of the system sequence and risk analysis; the right behavior depends on the design and the specific failure scenario.

## Why this matters
Assuming a valve will fail open or closed can misstate cooling behavior during a control or power event.

## Practical review points
- Check the approved sequence and valve documentation
- Identify which power or signal loss is considered
- Confirm alarms and fallback control behavior
- Document unresolved design assumptions

## Example from operations
During a controls upgrade, a team discovers conflicting drawings for a valve's fail position. The change is held for engineering review before programming proceeds.

## Frequently asked question
### Is fail-open always safer for a data center cooling valve?
No. Safe behavior depends on the system design, process conditions, and documented engineering intent.
