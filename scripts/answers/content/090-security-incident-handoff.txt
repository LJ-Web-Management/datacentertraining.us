## The direct answer
A security-to-operations handoff should preserve time, location, affected assets, access evidence, immediate controls, and the person responsible for follow-up without sharing unnecessary sensitive details.

## Why this matters
Poor handoffs can leave physical controls, equipment condition, or evidence preservation incomplete.

## Practical review points
- Use a defined notification path
- Record verified facts separately from assumptions
- Protect logs and restricted footage
- Assign action owner and review time

## Example from operations
A tailgating report involves a maintenance zone. Security informs operations of the location and access restriction while preserving badge events for review.

## Frequently asked question
### What should be included in a security incident handoff?
Enough verified information for operations to protect people and infrastructure and follow the incident plan, shared on a need-to-know basis.
