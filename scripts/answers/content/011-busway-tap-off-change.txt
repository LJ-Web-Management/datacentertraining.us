## The direct answer
A busway tap-off change should be reviewed for approved capacity, equipment compatibility, work authorization, load impact, and documentation before a qualified team performs the work.

## Why this matters
A small physical change can affect downstream protection, available capacity, and the accuracy of the facility's power records.

## Practical review points
- Confirm the exact busway and tap-off rating from approved records
- Review work authorization and isolation needs
- Coordinate load and change timing
- Update labeling and as-built records

## Example from operations
A new rack row is planned under an existing busway. The project engineer validates the tap-off design with the electrical authority before procurement.

## Frequently asked question
### Does a spare tap-off position mean spare electrical capacity?
No. Physical availability does not prove that upstream or downstream capacity and protection are adequate.
