## The direct answer
Warranty and service records should identify equipment, coverage dates, exclusions, response contacts, required maintenance, and the evidence needed to preserve support.

## Why this matters
A missed service condition or unclear coverage can delay repair and create disputes during a critical failure.

## Practical review points
- Connect each document to an asset ID
- Record coverage and expiration dates
- Track required maintenance evidence
- Review contacts and escalation path periodically

## Example from operations
A cooling unit begins recurring alarms near warranty expiration. The asset record gives the manager service terms and the correct support contact.

## Frequently asked question
### Does a warranty replace a preventive maintenance plan?
No. Follow the applicable maintenance requirements and track warranty terms separately.
