## The direct answer
Fan speed optimization should compare energy use with airflow distribution, rack inlet conditions, equipment limits, and stability across representative loads.

## Why this matters
Reducing fan speed based on energy alone can create a hidden cooling deficit at distant or high-density racks.

## Practical review points
- Establish baseline power and thermal readings
- Include farthest and highest-density racks
- Set stop criteria before a trial
- Compare equivalent load conditions

## Example from operations
A controls team proposes a lower fan-speed limit. Operations requests an instrumented trial with rack inlet monitoring and a defined rollback trigger.

## Frequently asked question
### Is lower fan speed always a cooling efficiency improvement?
No. The change must preserve required airflow and stable thermal conditions across the served area.
