## The direct answer
Parallel generator operation depends on coordinated electrical conditions and approved control logic; operators should understand indications and roles without making unauthorized switching decisions.

## Why this matters
Poorly coordinated switching can stress equipment, interrupt loads, or expose workers to electrical hazards.

## Practical review points
- Identify who is authorized to parallel units
- Review the approved sequence and communication roles
- Confirm relevant alarms are resolved before the event
- Record the operation and any deviation

## Example from operations
A planned test requires two units to operate in parallel. The facility manager verifies the approved test plan and assigns one lead for command and one observer for status logging.

## Frequently asked question
### Who should control generator paralleling?
The site's designated qualified personnel should control it under the approved operating procedure and manufacturer instructions.
