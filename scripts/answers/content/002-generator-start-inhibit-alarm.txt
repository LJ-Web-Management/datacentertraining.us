## The direct answer
A start inhibit means an expected automatic start may be blocked; operators should identify the active inhibit, confirm standby coverage, and escalate before relying on that generator.

## Why this matters
A generator that appears available but cannot start may reduce the site's real standby capacity during an outage.

## Practical review points
- Record the exact inhibit and time
- Check the approved equipment status source
- Confirm alternate capacity and escalation ownership
- Do not bypass an inhibit without authorization

## Example from operations
A weekly status review finds one unit marked ready while its controller displays an inhibit. The operator flags the mismatch for qualified maintenance and updates the shift record.

## Frequently asked question
### Can an operator clear a generator start inhibit?
Only personnel authorized by the site's procedure should investigate or clear an inhibit; never bypass protective logic to make a status screen appear normal.
