## The direct answer
An exercise review should distinguish observed facts from assumptions, assign corrective-action owners, set due dates, and verify that changes improve the plan or response capability.

## Why this matters
Without follow-up, an exercise can reveal the same weakness repeatedly without reducing exposure.

## Practical review points
- Capture decisions and delays
- Compare actions with intended plan
- Assign owner and completion evidence
- Retest material changes in a later exercise

## Example from operations
A tabletop reveals confusion about who notifies a customer after a power event. The review adds a role-specific action and tests the contact path later.

## Frequently asked question
### When is an exercise finding really closed?
When the agreed correction is implemented and the organization has evidence that it works as intended.
