## The direct answer
Seasonal readiness should focus on site-specific exposure, equipment dependencies, staffing, contractor coverage, utility arrangements, and emergency communication before conditions change.

## Why this matters
A plan that worked in one season may miss different cooling, drainage, access, or fuel constraints in another.

## Practical review points
- Review site hazard and weather scenarios
- Check equipment and service dependencies
- Verify contact and staffing coverage
- Assign action owners before the season starts

## Example from operations
Before a severe-weather period, a facility manager reviews roof access, fuel support, and staff callout coverage with the responsible teams.

## Frequently asked question
### Is a generic seasonal checklist enough for every data center?
No. Adapt the review to local hazards, design, contracts, and site response plans.
