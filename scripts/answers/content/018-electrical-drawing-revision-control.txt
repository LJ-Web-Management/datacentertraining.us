## The direct answer
Electrical work should use a controlled, current drawing set with a clear revision, owner, and change history; field markups need a defined path back into approved records.

## Why this matters
Stale drawings can misidentify sources and create unsafe assumptions during maintenance or future projects.

## Practical review points
- Verify revision before planning work
- Compare changes against field conditions
- Capture redlines with location and date
- Assign closeout owner for as-built updates

## Example from operations
A contractor arrives with a printed one-line from an earlier project phase. The project lead checks the current controlled set before the pre-task briefing.

## Frequently asked question
### Are marked-up drawings automatically approved as as-builts?
No. Markups are evidence for updating records, but they require review and acceptance through document control.
