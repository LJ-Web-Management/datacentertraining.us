## The direct answer
A refrigerant alarm response should define who acknowledges, who assesses from a safe location, who is qualified to service equipment, and how occupants are notified under the emergency plan.

## Why this matters
Confused roles can expose staff or delay the response to a release or equipment failure.

## Practical review points
- Use the posted response and notification matrix
- Keep unqualified staff outside restricted areas
- Contact trained refrigeration personnel
- Record alarm, actions, and clearance authority

## Example from operations
A detector reports a refrigerant condition in a mechanical area. The operator alerts the designated responder and follows occupancy instructions instead of entering to investigate.

## Frequently asked question
### Should a general operator investigate a refrigerant alarm inside the room?
No. Follow the site's emergency instructions and leave entry or service to personnel trained and authorized for that hazard.
