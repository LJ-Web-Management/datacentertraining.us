## The direct answer
Environmental records should connect equipment identity, service events, applicable materials, vendors, required documentation, and corrective actions so the facility can retrieve a coherent history.

## Why this matters
Dispersed records can make it difficult to verify what work occurred or whether follow-up was completed.

## Practical review points
- Assign equipment and record identifiers
- Collect vendor service documentation
- Track open issues and required reports
- Review access and retention with the compliance owner

## Example from operations
A facility audit asks for service history on a cooling asset. The equipment register links work orders, vendor reports, and environmental documentation.

## Frequently asked question
### Should every service record be stored only in an email inbox?
No. Use the organization's controlled recordkeeping system and assign an accountable owner.
