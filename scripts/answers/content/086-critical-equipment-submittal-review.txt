## The direct answer
A submittal review should compare proposed equipment and interfaces with the design basis, operating limits, maintainability, controls, and documentation requirements before procurement.

## Why this matters
A late mismatch can cause costly redesign or create a system that operations cannot support as planned.

## Practical review points
- Check ratings and interface assumptions
- Review service clearances and replacement path
- Confirm alarms and control points
- Record deviations and approval conditions

## Example from operations
A proposed unit meets the headline capacity but requires more service clearance than the room provides. The reviewer identifies the conflict before release.

## Frequently asked question
### Is matching the equipment capacity enough to approve a submittal?
No. Interfaces, serviceability, controls, and project requirements also need review.
