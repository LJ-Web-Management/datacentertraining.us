## The direct answer
Grounding and bonding reviews should use qualified personnel, approved criteria, and traceable records; visual observations alone cannot establish electrical integrity.

## Why this matters
Incomplete records or assumptions about bonding can mask gaps in protection and make future changes harder to assess.

## Practical review points
- Use current drawings and acceptance criteria
- Identify the equipment and connection points reviewed
- Record test method and instrument details when applicable
- Route anomalies to the electrical authority

## Example from operations
A rack relocation changes the bonding path. The project lead updates the drawing package and has the designated qualified person verify the installation before closeout.

## Frequently asked question
### Can a visual check confirm a grounding connection is adequate?
No. Visual review can identify obvious damage, but adequacy must be determined using approved methods by qualified personnel.
