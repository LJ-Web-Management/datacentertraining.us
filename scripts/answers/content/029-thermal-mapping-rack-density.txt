## The direct answer
Thermal mapping after density changes should focus on representative rack inlets, operating states, and known hot spots, then compare results with approved environmental criteria.

## Why this matters
Sparse or poorly placed sensors can miss localized heat that affects equipment reliability.

## Practical review points
- Define measurement locations and time window
- Capture rack-level inlet conditions
- Include different load and cooling states
- Document exceptions and follow-up actions

## Example from operations
A new high-density row is commissioned. The team records inlet temperatures across the row under a representative load before declaring the change complete.

## Frequently asked question
### Is one sensor at the room return enough to assess thermal conditions?
Usually not; it may not represent rack inlet conditions across different zones.
