## The direct answer
A roof review should connect drains, penetrations, flashing, access controls, and interior leak indicators so defects can be reported and prioritized before water reaches critical areas.

## Why this matters
A small roof defect may remain invisible until it threatens equipment or interrupts operations.

## Practical review points
- Use the approved roof access process
- Check drains and visible penetrations from safe areas
- Compare observations with interior leak alerts
- Document location and route repair to qualified contractors

## Example from operations
A ceiling sensor alarms after a rooftop project. Facilities compares the alarm location with the roof work record and routes inspection to the authorized roofing contractor.

## Frequently asked question
### Should facility operators climb onto a roof to investigate every leak alarm?
No. Use the site's access and fall-protection controls and qualified personnel for roof work.
