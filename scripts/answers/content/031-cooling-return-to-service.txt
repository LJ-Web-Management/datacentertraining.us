## The direct answer
Returning cooling equipment to service requires confirming maintenance closeout, control mode, alarms, flow or airflow evidence, and the responsible operator's acceptance under site procedure.

## Why this matters
A unit can appear running while controls, valves, or downstream conditions remain incorrect after work.

## Practical review points
- Review work completion and permits
- Confirm the approved operating mode
- Check alarms and available performance indicators
- Record acceptance and any temporary limitations

## Example from operations
A technician finishes a valve actuator replacement. Before closing the ticket, operations confirms command and feedback agree and the unit has returned to the documented sequence.

## Frequently asked question
### Does a green run indicator prove equipment is ready for service?
No. Confirm operating state and relevant system response, not only a single status point.
