**Short answer:** A water-intrusion review should map site exposure, drainage paths, vulnerable equipment, detection, access, and response ownership using the facility's risk assessment.

## Why it matters

A local leak or external water event can affect power, cooling, access, and business continuity at the same time.

## What to check

- Review site and room water pathways
- Identify critical equipment elevations and barriers
- Check detection and notification zones
- Assign inspection and response actions

## Example from the field

Heavy rain exposes a drainage issue near an electrical service entrance. The site plan gives facilities and security a shared inspection and escalation route.

## Follow-up question

### Does a raised equipment platform eliminate water risk?

No. Water can enter by several paths; controls should reflect site-specific exposure and response capability.
