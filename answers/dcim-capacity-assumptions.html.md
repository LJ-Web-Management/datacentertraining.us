**Short answer:** A capacity dashboard is only as reliable as its assumptions for rated limits, reserve margins, dependencies, and update frequency; teams should make those assumptions visible.

## Why it matters

A misleading utilization percentage can approve a load that the physical system cannot safely support.

## What to check

- Document source and date for limits
- Separate nameplate from usable capacity
- Show reserve and constraint assumptions
- Reconcile dashboard with approved engineering review

## Example from the field

A dashboard reports spare capacity while a maintenance restriction reduces available redundancy. The planner adds the constraint to the review before approving new load.

## Follow-up question

### Does a green DCIM capacity indicator guarantee a new deployment is acceptable?

No. Confirm constraints and dependencies with the responsible engineering and operations owners.
