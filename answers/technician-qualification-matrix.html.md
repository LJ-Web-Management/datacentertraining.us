**Short answer:** A qualification matrix should map each task to required knowledge, demonstrated capability, authorization, limits, and renewal triggers; course completion alone does not establish every work privilege.

## Why it matters

Confusing course attendance with authorization can assign work beyond a person's verified capability.

## What to check

- List tasks by hazard and equipment
- Define evidence required for each role
- Set approval and renewal owner
- Check matrix before assigning nonroutine work

## Example from the field

A newly hired technician completes online modules but has not observed a switching task. The supervisor assigns shadowing and evaluation before independent responsibility.

## Follow-up question

### Does completing a course authorize a technician to perform every related task?

No. Employers and site authorities define task authorization and any required qualifications.
