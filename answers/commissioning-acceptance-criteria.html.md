**Short answer:** Acceptance criteria should state the tested condition, expected result, tolerance or decision rule, evidence required, and who can accept a deviation before testing begins.

## Why it matters

If success is defined after the test, teams may interpret the same result differently or close a defect without agreement.

## What to check

- Write measurable criteria before testing
- Name reviewer and acceptance authority
- Define treatment of exceptions
- Link evidence to the test and equipment revision

## Example from the field

A test reaches the planned load but the report says only “satisfactory.” The commissioning lead reopens it for the agreed acceptance criteria and supporting measurements.

## Follow-up question

### Can a passing test be declared without preset criteria?

It is stronger and more repeatable to define criteria in advance; exceptions should be documented and approved by the designated authority.
