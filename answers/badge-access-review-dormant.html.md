**Short answer:** A badge review should compare active access with current job need, sponsorship, contract status, and last use while keeping a record of approval and removal decisions.

## Why it matters

Dormant credentials can remain usable after a project ends or a person's role changes.

## What to check

- Set review cadence and accountable owner
- Compare access to current work scope
- Investigate unused or conflicting privileges
- Record approvals and revocations

## Example from the field

A project badge remains active months after construction. The quarterly review confirms the sponsor has no current need and removes the access through the approved process.

## Follow-up question

### Does a badge that has not been used prove there is no risk?

No. Unused access can still be available; review it against current need and policy.
