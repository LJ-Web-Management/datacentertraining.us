**Short answer:** A humidity excursion review should capture the value, duration, location, sensor condition, and operating state, then compare them with the facility's chosen equipment criteria.

## Why it matters

A single snapshot may overstate or understate the actual exposure and can lead to poor corrective action.

## What to check

- Preserve trend interval and sensor identity
- Compare affected zones and redundant sensors
- Review door, economizer, and maintenance events
- Record decision and follow-up threshold

## Example from the field

One sensor briefly reports low humidity after a door was propped open. The team checks the trend and sensor location before escalating the event as a sustained excursion.

## Follow-up question

### Should one short humidity spike trigger a system-wide adjustment?

Not by itself. Validate the measurement and compare it with approved site criteria first.
