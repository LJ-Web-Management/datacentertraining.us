**Short answer:** A water-interruption plan should identify which systems depend on water, available reserve or alternate modes, decision authority, notification requirements, and the conditions for load protection.

## Why it matters

A building may retain electrical power yet lose cooling capacity or a service required for safe operation.

## What to check

- Map water-dependent equipment and limits
- Confirm vendor and utility notifications
- Define operational triggers and priorities
- Exercise the communication path

## Example from the field

A municipal notice announces a planned water interruption. Operations checks cooling dependencies and confirms the response with the site owner before the work date.

## Follow-up question

### Can a data center continue normally if electrical power is available during a water outage?

Not necessarily; evaluate all utility and cooling dependencies against the facility plan.
