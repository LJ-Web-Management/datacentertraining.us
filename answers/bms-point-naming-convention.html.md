**Short answer:** A point name should identify the system, location, equipment, and measured or commanded function consistently enough for operators to search and interpret it.

## Why it matters

Ambiguous names slow response and increase the chance that staff act on the wrong equipment.

## What to check

- Define a naming pattern and owner
- Include physical location and asset identity
- Avoid unexplained abbreviations
- Test names with operators who receive alarms

## Example from the field

Two sensors appear as “Temp 1” in different areas. The project team revises naming before turnover so operators can distinguish zone and rack location.

## Follow-up question

### Should naming conventions be designed only by controls engineers?

No. Operators and maintenance staff should verify that names support real troubleshooting tasks.
