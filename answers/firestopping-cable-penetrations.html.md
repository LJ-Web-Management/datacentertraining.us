**Short answer:** Penetration closeout should identify the location, approved system, installer or responsible party, inspection evidence, and any future maintenance restriction required by the facility.

## Why it matters

Unrecorded or altered firestopping can compromise fire compartmentation and make later cable additions difficult to control.

## What to check

- Map each penetration to a unique location
- Match installed system to approved submittal
- Record inspection and repair evidence
- Require review before later cable changes

## Example from the field

A new fiber route uses an existing sleeve. The project lead verifies capacity and approved firestop restoration and updates the penetration record.

## Follow-up question

### Can a team add cable through a sealed penetration without a new review?

No. Follow the approved system and facility's change process; restore and document protection as required.
