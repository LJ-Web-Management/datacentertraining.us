**Short answer:** Concurrent work review should identify shared systems, access constraints, simultaneous impairments, communication dependencies, and the single authority who can stop or sequence tasks.

## Why it matters

Two individually acceptable jobs can create a combined risk when they depend on the same reserve capacity or area.

## What to check

- Compare work plans on shared dependencies
- Mark simultaneous impairments and access blocks
- Set a coordination lead
- Reassess when either task scope changes

## Example from the field

A power test and cooling service are scheduled in the same hall. The coordinator checks their combined effect and changes the sequence before approving both windows.

### Can separate work permits be enough when jobs overlap?

Not always. The team should review interactions and dependencies across permits and work plans.
