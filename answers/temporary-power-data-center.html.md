**Short answer:** Temporary power near critical loads requires an approved plan that defines source, routing, capacity, protection, ownership, and removal before the connection is used.

## Why it matters

Poorly coordinated temporary power can introduce overload, trip hazards, undocumented dependencies, or unintended single points of failure.

## What to check

- Confirm authorization and load calculation
- Agree on routing and protection
- Label the source and responsible owner
- Set an inspection and removal date

## Example from the field

A construction crew requests a temporary feed during a fit-out. The facility team reviews the plan with the electrical lead and records the dependency before energization.

## Follow-up question

### Can a temporary power cable be routed wherever it is convenient?

No. Follow site-approved routing, electrical protection, and access rules; convenience does not establish suitability.
