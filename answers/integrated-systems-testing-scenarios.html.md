**Short answer:** Integrated testing should exercise documented interactions among power, cooling, controls, alarms, and people using approved scenarios and safety boundaries.

## Why it matters

A component may pass independently while a cross-system response fails or produces an unexpected consequence.

## What to check

- Start from credible failure scenarios
- Map system and human dependencies
- Define stop conditions and command roles
- Capture expected and actual sequence

## Example from the field

A utility event scenario includes a generator start, UPS response, and cooling control transition. The test plan assigns one person to command and another to log events.

## Follow-up question

### Should integrated testing try every conceivable failure at once?

No. Use engineered, approved scenarios with clear objectives, controls, and stop criteria.
