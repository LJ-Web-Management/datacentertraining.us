**Short answer:** A panel schedule is useful only when it matches verified field conditions and the current approved drawing set; discrepancies should be logged and resolved by authorized personnel.

## Why it matters

Incorrect circuit identification can delay isolation, misdirect maintenance, and undermine capacity planning.

## What to check

- Compare labels to controlled drawings
- Flag unknown or conflicting circuits
- Use qualified personnel for verification
- Record revision and approval date

## Example from the field

A maintenance request names a panel circuit that conflicts with the field label. The work is paused for an authorized verification instead of guessing from an old spreadsheet.

## Follow-up question

### Can staff simply relabel a circuit based on a rack name?

No. Labels should follow verified circuit identity and the site's document-control process.
