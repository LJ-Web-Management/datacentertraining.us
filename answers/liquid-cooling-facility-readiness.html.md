**Short answer:** A liquid-cooled deployment needs facility review of heat rejection, water or coolant interfaces, leak detection, maintenance ownership, emergency response, and monitoring before the row is accepted.

## Why it matters

A rack can be ready while the facility loop, alarms, or maintenance responsibilities are not.

## What to check

- Map facility and IT boundaries
- Verify monitoring and leak response ownership
- Review fluid quality and service responsibilities
- Document commissioning and operating limits

## Example from the field

A compute vendor declares a row ready but the facility has no agreed alarm recipient for secondary-loop leak detection. Handover stays open until ownership is assigned.

## Follow-up question

### Does a liquid-cooled rack only require IT-side readiness?

No. Facility infrastructure, monitoring, maintenance, and response arrangements are part of readiness.
