**Short answer:** A coolant leak response needs a shared boundary between rack, loop, and facility teams, with clear alarm ownership, escalation, isolation authority, and evidence preservation.

## Why it matters

Unclear responsibility can delay containment or lead to incompatible actions by separate teams.

## What to check

- Identify who owns each leak sensor
- Define who may isolate each loop segment
- Notify affected rack and operations owners
- Record actions and verify restart criteria

## Example from the field

A rack sensor alarms while the facility loop remains normal. The incident lead brings both teams into one response channel and follows the agreed isolation authority.

## Follow-up question

### Who should decide whether to isolate a cooling loop?

The designated facility authority under the incident procedure, coordinated with affected system owners.
