**Short answer:** A space adjacency review checks whether nearby rooms or routes introduce water, heat, fire, access, vibration, or maintenance risks to critical data center functions.

## Why it matters

An internal layout can create exposures even when the data hall itself meets its design criteria.

## What to check

- Map neighboring rooms and services
- Review shared walls, doors, drains, and penetrations
- Assess access and maintenance interactions
- Assign mitigation owner for material risks

## Example from the field

A janitorial sink is proposed beside a low-voltage room. The design reviewer checks leak pathways and access controls before accepting the layout.

## Follow-up question

### Should adjacency risk be reviewed only during initial design?

No. Renovations and use changes can introduce new neighboring hazards later.
