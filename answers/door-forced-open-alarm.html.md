**Short answer:** A forced-door alarm response should verify the door and area through approved monitoring, dispatch the assigned responder, preserve access logs, and escalate based on observed conditions.

## Why it matters

A delayed or uncoordinated response can weaken both facility safety and security accountability.

## What to check

- Confirm alarm location and time
- Follow dispatch and escort rules
- Keep access records and camera evidence
- Document door condition and corrective action

## Example from the field

A door alarm appears during a contractor delivery. Security checks the authorized delivery window and dispatches an escort to confirm the door was secured.

## Follow-up question

### Should a facilities technician ignore a forced-door alert if the door closes?

No. Follow the alarm response and document the verified cause and condition.
