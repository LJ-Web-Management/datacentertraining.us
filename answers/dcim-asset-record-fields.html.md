**Short answer:** A useful DCIM record connects an asset identity to location, owner, dependencies, capacity context, maintenance state, and current documentation so staff can act from one trusted reference.

## Why it matters

Incomplete records slow response and can cause teams to miss the dependencies behind an apparently local issue.

## What to check

- Use consistent asset identifiers
- Assign owner and criticality
- Link current drawings and work history
- Review fields when equipment moves or changes

## Example from the field

A technician searches a panel by nickname and finds multiple assets. The team standardizes identifiers so tickets, alarms, and drawings point to the same equipment.

## Follow-up question

### Should a DCIM record replace controlled engineering documents?

No. It can point users to controlled documents but should not become an uncontrolled substitute.
