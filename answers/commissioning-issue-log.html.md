**Short answer:** An issue log should include unique ID, location, consequence, owner, due date, evidence needed for closure, and acceptance authority, with open items visible through handover.

## Why it matters

A defect described only in meeting notes can lose ownership or be accepted without a verified fix.

## What to check

- Use one controlled issue register
- Prioritize by consequence and dependency
- Require evidence and reviewer for closure
- Transfer remaining limitations to operations explicitly

## Example from the field

A sensor mapping issue is repeatedly discussed but not assigned. The commissioning manager adds an owner and closeout test before the system is accepted.

## Follow-up question

### Can an issue be closed because the contractor says it is fixed?

No. Closure should follow verification against the agreed requirement and recorded evidence.
