**Short answer:** Electrical room access should be limited to people whose role and authorization require it, with visitors controlled under the facility's access and escort process.

## Why it matters

Uncontrolled entry can expose unqualified people to hazards and weaken accountability during maintenance or incidents.

## What to check

- Define approved roles and access duration
- Verify identity and work purpose
- Record escort and entry events
- Remove access when assignments end

## Example from the field

A vendor requests a badge for repeated visits but has no scheduled work order. The coordinator confirms the sponsor and scope before granting time-limited access.

## Follow-up question

### Does a facility badge make someone qualified to work on electrical equipment?

No. Physical access and task qualification are separate controls; verify both through site processes.
