**Short answer:** A displayed UPS runtime is an estimate based on current system inputs and assumptions, not a guaranteed duration; teams should interpret it with load, battery condition, and approved response criteria.

## Why it matters

Treating one estimate as a promise can create false confidence during a power event.

## What to check

- Record timestamp and load context
- Compare with current battery health information
- Use site-defined thresholds and escalation
- Avoid extrapolating beyond validated conditions

## Example from the field

During a utility event, runtime estimates change as load shifts. The incident lead reports the trend and follows the site's response triggers rather than quoting one number as certain.

## Follow-up question

### Is the UPS runtime display an exact prediction?

No. It is an estimate that depends on conditions and should be interpreted using approved operational criteria.
