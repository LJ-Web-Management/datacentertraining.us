**Short answer:** Handover training should explain normal operation, alarms, dependencies, maintenance boundaries, unusual conditions, vendor support, and the controlled documents operators will use.

## Why it matters

A facility can be technically complete while the receiving team lacks practical knowledge to operate or maintain it.

## What to check

- Identify each equipment owner and user role
- Include abnormal and recovery scenarios
- Capture questions and unresolved limits
- Confirm training materials match installed configuration

## Example from the field

A controls contractor conducts a generic demonstration using an earlier point map. The owner requests a session based on the final installed configuration.

## Follow-up question

### Is a vendor demonstration enough for operational handover?

Not by itself. Confirm it covers the site's roles, procedures, current configuration, and evidence needs.
