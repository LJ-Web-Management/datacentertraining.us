**Short answer:** Utility restoration is a controlled transition that requires source confirmation, clear command authority, load awareness, and documented status checks under the facility's approved sequence.

## Why it matters

A rushed return can create confusion about which source is carrying load and whether standby systems are ready for the next event.

## What to check

- Confirm source stability through approved indicators
- Assign a single operational lead
- Coordinate with generator and UPS status owners
- Record the sequence and final state

## Example from the field

After a prolonged outage, the utility reports service restored. The facility lead follows the restoration plan and confirms status across the power chain before closing the event.

## Follow-up question

### Should teams transfer back as soon as utility power returns?

Only when the approved criteria and authorized decision process say the source is suitable and the transition is safe.
