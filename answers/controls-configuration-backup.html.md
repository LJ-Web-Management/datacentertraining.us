What to capture before changing BMS or controls logic: the current approved configuration, target revision, rollback plan, and validation evidence.

**Short answer:** Before changing control logic, teams should preserve the current approved configuration, identify the target revision, and define rollback and validation evidence.

## Why it matters

Without a known baseline, recovery may be slow or the team may restore an unapproved version.

## What to check

- Verify backup completeness and date
- Record change owner and scope
- Protect backup access and integrity
- Test rollback method in an approved context

## Example from the field

A point mapping change is planned after a sensor replacement. The controls lead captures the current configuration and states how the change will be verified.

## Follow-up question

### Is a screenshot enough to back up a control system?

Usually not; use the system's approved backup and version-control process, with evidence that it can be restored.
