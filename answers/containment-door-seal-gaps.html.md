**Short answer:** Containment gaps should be assessed for airflow impact, location, and the facility's intended pressure and temperature strategy; repairs should preserve access and life-safety requirements.

## Why it matters

Small bypass paths can mix supply and return air and make cooling performance harder to predict.

## What to check

- Map the gap and affected row
- Check temperature and pressure trends nearby
- Use approved materials and fire/life-safety review
- Recheck conditions after repair

## Example from the field

A door closer leaves a persistent gap at one end of a row. The facilities lead logs it and checks nearby rack inlet trends before assigning repair.

## Follow-up question

### Should every gap be sealed immediately with any available material?

No. Repair materials and access controls should be compatible with facility, fire, and manufacturer requirements.
