**Short answer:** Plant staging should be evaluated against current load, equipment operating limits, redundancy requirements, and the approved control sequence rather than energy use alone.

## Why it matters

An efficiency change that reduces available reserve can increase outage risk or cause unstable operation.

## What to check

- Review load and equipment status
- Confirm minimum redundancy criteria
- Compare sequence with observed response
- Require change approval and rollback plan

## Example from the field

A control proposal delays starting a chiller to reduce low-load cycling. The operations team checks the reserve requirement and temperature response before approving a trial.

## Follow-up question

### Should the most energy-efficient staging sequence always be used?

No. Efficiency must be balanced with reliability, operating limits, and the facility's design criteria.
