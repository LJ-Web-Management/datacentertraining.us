**Short answer:** Contractor photography rules should be explained before entry, tied to the work scope, and enforced through the site's approval and device-control process.

## Why it matters

Unapproved images can expose sensitive facility details or create uncertainty about what information left the site.

## What to check

- State restrictions before issuing access
- Define approved exceptions and sponsor
- Explain device and storage rules
- Record acknowledgement when required

## Example from the field

A technician takes a phone photo to document a repair. The escort checks policy and routes the image through approved documentation instead of assuming the photo is acceptable.

## Follow-up question

### Can a contractor take photos for a work order without asking?

Follow site policy and sponsor approval; technical need does not automatically create permission.
