**Short answer:** An SOP describes repeatable normal work, an MOP controls a defined change or maintenance task, and an EOP guides response to an abnormal or emergency condition; local terminology may vary.

## Why it matters

Using the wrong document type can leave responders without the decision points or approvals their situation requires.

## What to check

- Define document types in site policy
- Label current approved version clearly
- Match review rigor to task risk
- Train staff where the documents are stored

## Example from the field

A routine filter check is mistakenly attached to an emergency response plan. The document owner separates routine steps from abnormal-condition actions.

## Follow-up question

### Are these procedure names universal across all operators?

No. Organizations may use different terms, so the site's document-control definitions govern.
