**Short answer:** Economizer operation should account for the designed operating envelope and relevant outdoor conditions, with control decisions based on approved sensors and site-specific risk criteria.

## Why it matters

Outdoor conditions can change thermal performance and may introduce contaminants or moisture concerns depending on the system design.

## What to check

- Review permitted operating conditions
- Verify sensor location and data quality
- Check filtration and maintenance status
- Trend indoor conditions during mode changes

## Example from the field

An economizer enters a new mode during a dusty weather event. The facility team checks the approved environmental criteria and verifies the response through monitoring.

## Follow-up question

### Can outside air be used whenever it is cooler than return air?

No. Temperature is only one consideration; the design's environmental and equipment limits control.
